# Cat joke candidates (draft pass)

One line each, under 200 characters: a single joke about
cats that lands.

1. Keyboard review: "My cat sat on my keyboard to help with
   my novel; she deleted nothing, but her review was clear."
   -- implied criticism; could be out-landed by the meeting
   joke's management structure.
2. The meeting: "My cat called a family meeting by sitting
   in the suitcase: agenda, one item; decision, also one." --
   the suitcase as agenda is a complete story, and every cat
   owner has attended this meeting.
3. Rent: "The cat pays no rent, eats first, and supervises
   all work from a warm laptop: she is not a pet, she is
   management." -- the management frame again, warmer.
4. 3 a.m.: "At 3 a.m. my cat runs the hallway marathon to
   remind me the house has a night shift and I am not it." --
   energetic, visual.
5. Box: "I bought the cat a bed shaped like a castle; she
   sleeps in the box it came in, like a monarch in exile." --
   the classic box truth, dressed up.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The keyboard review (candidate 1) is
implied criticism; the suitcase meeting is a complete
governance structure in one image -- the cat convenes the
family by occupying the luggage, the agenda has one item,
the decision has one item, and everyone present understands
both. It lands because every cat owner has attended this
exact meeting.
